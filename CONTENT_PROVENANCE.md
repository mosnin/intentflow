# Content provenance

The repository keeps two separate content layers:

- `content/pages.json` and `content/structured-pages.json` are archival captures of the public `osinoffgrp.com` page copy. They are retained only for traceability and are not rendered by the website.
- `content/published-pages.json` is the only content dataset imported by the application. It is generated by `scripts/publish_content.py` and removes quantitative campaign claims, pricing and commission language, placeholder testimonials, and claims of verification that have not been independently approved.

The homepage product preview and the industry autocomplete examples are explicitly labeled as illustrative. They do not present campaign totals, customer identities, live account state, or guaranteed outcomes.

Before publishing new proof, add the approved copy to the publishing layer and run:

```sh
npm run content:publish
npm run content:check
```

Do not render the archival capture directly.
