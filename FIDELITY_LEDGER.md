## Content integrity decisions

- Raw crawl files are archival inputs and are never rendered directly.
- Quantitative performance, pricing, commission, and contractual claims require separately approved evidence before publication.
- Case-study totals, customer quotes, and outcome tables are withheld pending approval.
- The hero mockup is explicitly illustrative and contains no campaign or client data.
- Assessment and discovery routes use the published email and phone contacts instead of simulated submissions.

- Published-content integrity check: passed for all 20 routes

## Intentional deviation

The purchased template's data-heavy dashboard was converted into a clearly labeled conceptual search journey. This preserves the licensed visual composition without presenting fabricated business results as real information.
